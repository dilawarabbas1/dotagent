# Code of Conduct

dotagent follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

Be respectful. Disagree on technical merit, not personal grounds. Assume
good faith. Don't harass anyone for any reason.

## Reporting

Open a confidential issue on the GitHub repo or email the maintainer at the
address in `pyproject.toml`. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may, at their discretion, edit, lock, or remove any contribution
or contributor whose behavior violates this code.
